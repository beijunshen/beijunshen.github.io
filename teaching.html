Teaching

Mechanics is the subject I most enjoy teaching, because a student can feel it before deriving it: why a helmet liner cushions an impact, why a rubber band stiffens near its limit, why a plastic film tears easily in one direction. I teach physical intuition first and the formalism second, derive on the board, give every abstraction a drawing, and use computation as a way of thinking that is checked against theory and experiment.

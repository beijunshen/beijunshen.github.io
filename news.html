News and Activities

September 2026 — Excited to share that my first postdoc work with Prof. Mary C. Boyce is now on arXiv, after countless rounds of iteration and revision: “Composition-dependent nonlinear viscoelastic–viscoplastic behavior and constitutive framework for digitally mixed polymers spanning the glass transition”. One finite-strain constitutive framework now describes the whole family of digitally mixed polymers, from rubbery to glassy, and predicts a mixture we held back from calibration. It sets up the fundamental tool for understanding these materials and a predictive basis for designing graded multi-material structures. Very proud of this one. Our collaborative paper on data-driven discovery of composition-dependent constitutive models for the same materials is also out. More papers are on the way, stay tuned!

July 2026 — I was very honored to be invited to give a presentation at the Third Advances in Applied Mechanics Conference (AAM 2027), Madeira, Portugal, January 2027 (declined). I am very grateful for this recognition of my work.

June 2026 — I attended the 20th U.S. National Congress of Theoretical and Applied Mechanics (USNCTAM 2026) in Pasadena, CA, and gave an oral presentation, “From Digital Multi-Materials to Bio-Inspired Gradients: Mechanics-Guided Design of Compositionally Mixed Soft Materials.” The work introduces the new concept of time–composition equivalence and develops a unified constitutive framework spanning the full spectrum from elastomeric to glassy behavior. It truly felt like home to be part of such an inspiring mechanics community, and the discussions afterward sparked many new ideas. We are currently wrapping up the related papers — stay tuned!
